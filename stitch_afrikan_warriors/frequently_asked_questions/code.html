Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking, requirements, and the unparalleled cinematic heritage experience of the Afrikan Warriors.

Do you offer discounts for multi-day bookings? add
Yes, we offer tailored packages for residencies, festivals, and multi-day events. The discount structure depends on the duration of the engagement and the scale of the required performances. Please contact us with your event details for a custom quote.
Are travel and accommodation costs included? add
No, all international and domestic travel, freight for specialized equipment, and appropriate accommodation must be covered by the client in addition to the performance fee unless explicitly negotiated in a comprehensive package.
Do you accept seasonal or residency contracts? add
Absolutely. The Afrikan Warriors frequently undertake extended residencies at theme parks, grand hotels, and permanent theatrical venues globally. We require minimum 3-month commitments for international residencies.
What types of venues are suitable for your shows? add
Our kinetic performances are designed for high-impact visibility. We regularly perform in traditional circuses, large-scale theme parks, premium hotel atriums, and grand theatrical stages. Open-air amphitheaters are also suitable, subject to weather conditions.
How much is a short performance? add
Short, high-intensity showcase performances (typically 10-15 minutes) start at a premium base rate. Pricing scales based on the number of performers requested, specific act inclusions (like fire), and the prestige of the event.
What is your cancellation policy? add
A non-refundable 50% deposit secures the date. Cancellations made less than 30 days prior to the event require full payment. Exceptions are made solely for documented force majeure events.
Can we use photos/videos for promotion? add
Pre-approved press kits and imagery are provided for promotional use. Live recording during the performance is permitted for non-commercial social media use only, unless commercial broadcast rights are explicitly negotiated.
What happens if it rains during an outdoor show? add
Our athletic routines require a dry, non-slip surface for safety. For outdoor events, the client must provide an adequate weather contingency plan (covered stage or indoor alternative). If conditions are deemed unsafe by our lead performer, the show may be delayed or cancelled without refund.
Do you accept short-notice bookings? add
While we prefer a minimum 60-day lead time for international logistics, we can occasionally accommodate short-notice bookings within a 14-day window, subject to availability and expedited visa/travel arrangements (which will incur premium surcharges).
Can we choose the costume style? add
Our aesthetic is rooted in 'Cinematic Heritage'—premium, tribal-inspired performance wear. Clients can select from our curated wardrobe tiers (Traditional, Modern Brutalist, or Golden Gala) to best suit their event's theme.
What is required for the Limbo Fire option? add
The Limbo Fire act requires prior written safety approval from the venue, appropriate fire suppression equipment on standby, a minimum 4-meter clearance from all flammable materials/audience, and adequate ventilation if performed indoors.
What is the required performance space? add
For a standard kinetic showcase, we require a minimum flat, unobstructed, non-slip performance area of 5m x 5m (16ft x 16ft), with a minimum ceiling clearance of 4 meters for acrobatic maneuvers.
