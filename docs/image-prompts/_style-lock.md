# Afrikan Warriors — image style lock

Paste this with every prompt. Do not skip it. This is the difference between a black-box studio shot and a real theatre still.

## Brand

Cinematic Heritage. Luxury-minimal UI sitting on **high-detail live-theatre photography**. Gold light piercing a dark house. Tanzanian-born African acrobatic company. Athletic, ancestral, premium — not safari, not carnival kitsch, not Afrofuturist chrome.

## Palette

- Stage / void: `#0B0B0B` matte black
- Gold light: `#D4AF37` / `#f2ca50` warm theatrical amber, never neon, never cold white as the key
- Skin and earth: deep browns, ochre, copper, cowrie cream
- Accents in costume only: kente geometry, raffia, beadwork — restrained, not rainbow clutter

## Theatre (required on almost every frame)

Build a **house**, not a void:

- Dark pleated house curtains or tabs in the background
- Thick theatrical haze / fog catching the beams (volume, not a wispy CGI mist)
- **Several** warm lanterns: key from above-front, two side fills, optional backlight — not a single cone in empty space
- Dark scored wooden stage, slight sheen, dust in the air
- Audience heads as silhouettes in the foreground on wide heroes and production stills
- Real rigging / a ladder / a lighting barrel in the deep background is allowed if it stays dark

## People

- East African / Tanzanian adult performers, athletic, real bodies, sweat, muscle, skin texture
- Barefoot unless the act needs otherwise
- Costumes: dark performance wear, raffia or patterned wraps, gold/ochre geometric beadwork, cowrie, copper — theatrical and real
- Serious focus or performance energy. No stock-photo smiles. No fashion-editorial blank stares unless the slot is a character close-up.

## Camera

Photoreal still. 35mm or 50mm performance photography. IMAX / National Geographic × West End. Sharp subject, haze in the beams, crushed blacks at the edges, natural vignette. High contrast, not HDR plastic.

## Do not

No text, letters, logos, watermarks, UI, captions, or “AFRIKAN WARRIORS” in the image.  
No extra limbs, fused hands, broken pyramids, extra fingers.  
No cartoon, anime, illustration, 3D render, plastic CGI skin.  
No empty cyclorama, no one-spotlight sports-studio void.  
No fantasy armor, superhero costumes, chrome, neon cyberpunk.  
No wildlife, savannah stock, tourist cliché, tribal-mask Halloween.  
No identifiable real celebrities.

## Negative prompt (copy with every generation)

`text, letters, typography, logo, watermark, UI, caption, poster title, extra limbs, extra fingers, fused bodies, deformed anatomy, broken pyramid, cartoon, anime, illustration, 3D render, plastic skin, CGI gloss, empty black studio, cyclorama, single spotlight in a void, neon, cyberpunk, safari, wildlife, tourist cliché, celebrity lookalike, smiling stock photo, low detail, muddy lighting`
