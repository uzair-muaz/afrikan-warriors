## Prompt

Follow Afrikan Warriors cinematic-heritage grade: photoreal live-theatre still, not a studio void. Matte black house (#0B0B0B), warm gold theatrical light (#D4AF37 / amber), crushed blacks, natural vignette. East African / Tanzanian adult athletic performers, real sweat and skin texture, barefoot unless the act needs otherwise. Dark pleated house curtains, thick stage haze with visible volumetric beams from SEVERAL warm lanterns (key + two side fills + optional backlight), scored wooden stage with a slight sheen, dust in the air. Costumes: raffia or patterned wraps, gold/ochre geometric beadwork, cowrie, copper — theatrical and real, not carnival kitsch, not fantasy armor. Serious performance energy. No text, logos, or UI in the frame.

Wide 16:9 photograph from the stalls of a dark West End–scale theatre. Perfectly CENTRED composition: a troupe of about ten to twelve East African male acrobats forming a tall four-tier human pyramid on the exact midline of the frame — equal black curtains left and right, the flyer at the peak with arms wide, directly under the hottest lantern. Base four, then three, then two, then one. Shirtless athletic bodies, raffia / animal-print performance wraps over dark shorts, cowrie belts, gold geometric beadwork.

House: floor-to-ceiling dark pleated curtains. THREE distinct warm-gold spotlights raking through thick stage fog so the beams are solid cones. Smoke pooling around the feet. Dark wooden stage, slight reflection. Silhouettes of audience heads along the bottom edge of the frame, out of focus. Side fill kissing shoulders. High detail in muscle, fabric weave, curtain folds, and particulate in the haze.

Keep the lower third a little darker so white/gold website type can sit over it. Do not offset the pyramid to the side.
