## Prompt

Follow Afrikan Warriors cinematic-heritage grade: photoreal live-theatre still, not a studio void. Matte black house (#0B0B0B), warm gold theatrical light (#D4AF37 / amber), crushed blacks, natural vignette. East African / Tanzanian adult athletic performers, real sweat and skin texture, barefoot unless the act needs otherwise. Dark pleated house curtains, thick stage haze with visible volumetric beams from SEVERAL warm lanterns (key + two side fills + optional backlight), scored wooden stage with a slight sheen, dust in the air. Costumes: raffia or patterned wraps, gold/ochre geometric beadwork, cowrie, copper — theatrical and real, not carnival kitsch, not fantasy armor. Serious performance energy. No text, logos, or UI in the frame.

Vertical 4:5 cinematic close-up, perfectly SYMMETRIC and CENTRED. Two East African men, chest-up, in profile facing each other almost nose-to-nose: rival heirs of Zaramu. Intense, unsmiling, sweat, pores, braided hair. Left: raised temple scarification, long spear with decorated shaft. Right: ochre war paint across brow, carved wooden club. Heavy layered beads, cowrie, hammered copper neck-plate, woven geometric cloth on shoulders.

Hard warm theatrical side-light, gold on the inner faces, backs falling to black. Soft haze. Shallow depth. Macro detail on wood grain, metal, bead, skin. Background is dark curtain, not a void cyclorama — you should still feel fabric.
